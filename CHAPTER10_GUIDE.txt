Resident Evil 4 Remake - Chapter 10 guide (Leon)
Written by Claude, 11/14. UPDATE 11/21: Claude PLAYED all of Chapter 10 and N now has an exact step for every place
(new file lua/RE4Access/ch10.lua). Just press N each time. Enemy tips: Downloads\RE4R_Chapter10_Enemy_Tips.txt.
Two things learned in the game: the LOW counterweight is only visible from the SOUTH balcony (N takes you there),
and in the dark caves after the fall there is no map data, so N says the direction and you press N again.
In the game: press N. Where N has no exact spot yet, it reads this guide for your objective.

PART 1: Objective "Head for the throne room"

1. N: the ladder down to the Grand Hall, about 8 meters from where you start. Press F to kick it down, then F to climb down. This step is mapped.
2. Optional: the Merchant beside the Chimera door.
   - New stock.
   - A request called Merciless Knight: a gold armor knight in the mausoleum, with two normal knights. Flash grenades stun all three. Shoot the parasite when the helmet comes off. They drop gems.
3. Optional: in the library, the Cubic Device opens a box with the CQBR Assault Rifle. A second box in the study, the typewriter room upstairs, has the Golden Lynx treasure.
4. Way on: through the double doors past the Chimera statue. Giant bugs attack, and the shotgun works well.
5. The main door ahead is locked. Hop the railing on the left, kill two more bugs, and climb the stairs.
6. A big room. There is a First Aid Spray in the glass cabinet left of the entrance. Leave by the door on the right.
7. Stairs down. A swarm of bugs comes. Fight them from the space under the stairs so only a few reach you at a time.
8. The ballroom. The gate at the far east end needs 2 levers:
   - one on the upper balcony, at the end;
   - one at the stairs on the south side.
   Treasure: a Ruby in a hanging cage over the rubble in the middle (shoot the cage).
9. The drawbridge. Shoot 2 counterweights. The second one is hidden low behind the wall: step back and to the left to reach it.
10. The antechamber, a fight. 2 Garradors plus zealots who keep coming.
   - Garradors are blind and follow sound.
   - Shoot a bell: a Garrador charges it and gets stuck. Then hit the parasite on its back. Each bell works once.
   - Hand grenades are good here.
11. Each Garrador drops a Unicorn Horn. Put both horns in the statues on each side of the gate at the back.
12. Go through that gate to the throne room. A scene plays: Leon falls into the depths.

PART 2: Objective "Make your way to the surface"

13. The depths. Follow the water path. Bug enemies hide in the water.
14. A crack in a wall with light behind it: break through. An Elegant Crown, a treasure, lies on a body there. Take the Yellow Herb, then climb the ladder.
15. A tunnel. Shoot the hanging cage in the first alcove on the left for a Red Beryl.
16. The sewers. The Merchant and a typewriter: save here.
17. Liquid nitrogen storage. Read the note on the table. At the fork turn right, go into the first side door, and pull the lever.
18. The laboratory.
   - Tentacles burst from the floor: keep moving.
   - In the square room, go to the far right corner and pull the power switch.
19. Verdugo, a boss.
   - Run to a green switch on the wall: it sprays liquid nitrogen and freezes him. Then hit him.
   - Turn the wheel on the exit door and head for the elevator.
   - You can freeze him again at the nitrogen showers on the way, or just survive until the elevator comes.
   - Killing him gives the Gold Monocle, worth a lot of money.
20. Get into the elevator. The chapter ends.
