RE4 Access - Chapter 11 guide (the mines)
Played start to end with the mod on 11/26/2026. Press N at any time: it names the next step
and walks you there. These notes say the same in one place.

1. Start: you come out of the elevator. Press N: it walks you up a ladder (hold F 1 second
   at the ladder) to a painted rock blocking a tunnel. You need Dynamite for it.
   N goes on to the quarry by itself.

2. The quarry: you jump down a ledge (tap F at the edge). Villagers attack, a chainsaw man
   among them. Hold L and press K. Then N walks you up into a small hut with a control panel.
   The lever there: HOLD F down for 1 second. A bridge lowers.
   If F picks up money lying there, hold F again.

3. N takes you out of the hut, over the bridge, to the Dynamite on some boxes: press F, then
   F again to close the item screen.

4. N takes you back to the painted rock: press F, Enter on Dynamite, Enter on Use.
   Then press N AT ONCE and hold Shift and W: N runs you out of the blast along a safe path.
   The blast comes after 6 seconds.

5. Past the tunnel: a story event (press F), then two giants.
   - First the giant WITHOUT armor: keep your distance, hold L, press K.
   - Then the armored giant: keep 8 to 14 meters away (when it comes close, run with
     Shift and W, then turn back). Hold L: the mod aims at the padlock on its armor (or the
     explosive on its back if Luis planted one). Press K again and again until it dies.

6. Boost Luis over a wall: hold F 1 second. He opens the gate. N walks you to the mine cart:
   hold F 1 second to get in.

7. Mine cart ride 1: when the mod says Lean A or Lean D, hold that key until it says Straight.
   Hold L and press K on every track switch, barricade and red barrel it calls.

8. A short stop, then the second mine cart (N, hold F 1 second).
   Ride 2: the CHAINSAW MAN rides beside you. Keep L held the whole time and press K about
   3 times a second. He must die before the end of the long stretch, or he wrecks the cart
   and you die even at full health. The mod tells you his health every 3 seconds.
   Also shoot the barricade, the red barrels and the track switch near the end.

9. The cart crashes into a cave with insect hives (the yellow sacks: ignore them).
   N walks you to the elevator button: hold F 1 second. A long cutscene follows.

10. Krauser, knife only: hold Space and press K again and again, stay close. When the mod
    says Evade or Lunge, press the key it names. Press U for his health. When he is down,
    the chapter ends.
