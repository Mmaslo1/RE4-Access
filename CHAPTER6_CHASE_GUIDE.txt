RE4 Access - Chapter 6 chase, "Flee from the villagers"  (updated 11/1/2026)
Everything here is a key you press or words the mod says.

WHAT IT IS
This is a chase, not a boss fight. Chief Mendez is right behind you.
If he grabs you, you die. Pressing Space did not save you in the
2 grabs in your log, and neither did shooting.

WHAT TO DO
1. The mod says: "Run! Hold Shift and W."
2. Hold Shift and W, and keep holding both. You do not need to press N.
   The route starts by itself and the mod steers you.
3. Do not press L or K.
4. If the mod says "Grab coming! Keep running.": keep holding both keys.
5. If the mod says "You stopped.": hold Shift and W again.
6. If the mod says "Ashley is behind you": let go of W for a moment,
   then hold Shift and W again.
7. The chase is over when the mod says "New objective".
   Then press N for the next step.

HONEST NOTE
The mod knows only the end of this chase, not the exact path, and the
new steering has not been tried in the game yet. Your next run records
the path so Claude can add it.

THE BOSS AFTER IT (Mendez)
1. Hold L: the mod aims at him. Press K again and again.
2. When the mod says "F ready": press F.
3. When the mod says "Close": hold S to back away, then aim again.
4. Health low: press I to open the case. Heal there.

Press O at any time to hear the chase tips again.
