RESIDENT EVIL 4 - REST OF CHAPTER 7, AFTER THE MERCHANT

This comes from a Push Square walkthrough. It is not checked in your game yet,
and the mod's N key does not know these steps yet.

You are at step 1.

1. Audience Chamber. After the Merchant, a story scene with Salazar plays.
   Then a fight with Zealots, some with shields. Hand grenades help. Large pots
   have supplies. Then boost Ashley up in the northeast corner so she can unlock
   a metal gate. Take the Elegant Perfume Bottle, it sells to the Merchant.

2. Dungeons. A Ruby is on the left past a crack in the wall. The right path leads
   to the Dungeons. Go left through the prison and take the Dungeon Key.
   Leon falls down to a Garrador. It is blind, so stay quiet, sneak up behind it,
   and shoot the parasite on its back. It drops a Red Beryl.

3. Way back. A crank and a ladder lead back to the Dungeons entrance. The Dungeon
   Key opens the locked door. Climb the ladder to the storage room for a Clockwork
   Castellan and a Spinel.

4. Treasury. A sword puzzle, then a Gold Bangle and supplies.

5. Audience Chamber, upper floor. Kick down a ladder. Swing across on the
   chandeliers to a small room with a Golden Hourglass. Shoot the symbol plates
   on the left and right to open the metal gate to the west.

6. Water Hall. There is a typewriter just before it. Kill the Zealots on both
   levels. Take the Halo Wheel from its pedestal, use it on the machine to get back
   upstairs, then use it on the middle pedestal to lower the drawbridge.

7. Upper floor. Use the Halo Wheel on the machine at the end. Ashley then turns
   two cranks to raise the bridge while Zealots attack her from both sides.
   Protect her. This is the last big fight of the chapter.

8. End of chapter. When Ashley is back with you, cross the bridge and boost her
   over the outside wall.

OPTIONAL: Blue Medallions 3 at the gate area, before you go into the castle.
