RESIDENT EVIL 4 - CHAPTER 9 - THE HEDGE MAZE  (mapped by Claude 11/11)

Press N at every step. N speaks the next place, you hold W to walk there.

The order N will give you:
1. First Aid Spray, down in the maze close to the start. Press F to pick it up.
   (There is also a typewriter right at the start room, if you want to save first.)
2. The middle flag lever. Up a ladder, on top of the hedges. Press F.
3. The far flag lever, at the north-east end of the maze. Press F.
4. The lever Ashley holds, at the west side. Press F. Ashley holds it down
   and a gate opens.
5. Walk through the gate Ashley holds open, going north.
6. The first flag lever. Press F. Now all 3 flags are up.
7. The door the flags opened. Go through it, going east.
8. The castle gate, then past it.
9. A locked door with a slide lock. Press F to slide the lock, then go through.
10. N then says "End of my map. Tell Claude."
    The castle part after this was not loaded in the game yet, so I could not map it.

Danger: wolves (Colmillos) jump out of the hedges and out of cages.
You have little health. Use the First Aid Spray when health gets low.

Nothing here has been walked yet. If N says "Stop here" or "Blocked",
tell Claude what it said.

CASTLE (added 11/11 after Claude walked it)
- Castle doors open by HOLDING W and walking into them. F does nothing on them.
- The hall-end door leads to the DINING HALL (autosave there).
- Dining hall puzzle: 8 chairs with bells. Ashley sits in one right chair, you in the other.
  N: "Ashley's chair" - hold Left Control until Ashley answers.
  N: "your chair" - press F to sit, then F to ring.
- That opens a case with a Serpent head. N takes you to it (F), then to the statue in the hall (F).
