RE4 ACCESS - WHAT THE FIGHT WORDS MEAN, AND WHAT TO PRESS

SOUNDS
- Two low thuds = an enemy is close or in your way. Fight or back off.
- No thuds = you can walk or run.

WORDS
- "3 enemies"          How many are near you. Just information.
- "Close, ahead left"  An enemy is within 3 meters, in that direction.
                       Do: hold L and press K (shoot), or Space + K (knife),
                       or step away with S.
- "Locked"             Your aim is ON an enemy now (you are holding L).
                       Do: press K to shoot. Keep holding L.
- "Hit"                Your shot hit. Keep shooting (K) until "Enemy down".
- "Enemy down"         It fell or died. Next one.
- "F ready: kick the enemy"
                       The enemy is stunned. Do: press F NOW. The kick knocks
                       down everyone around you and costs no bullets.
                       Tip: shoot an enemy in the face or leg first; then this comes.
- "Lunge, ahead right" An enemy is jumping at you to grab you.
                       Do: move away to the side at once: hold A or D (with Shift = faster).
- "Grabbed! Space"     It caught you. Do: press Space right away (knife free).
- "Hit, health 30"     You got hurt; your health is now 30 percent.
- "Dynamite! left"     An enemy is lighting dynamite.
                       Do: hold L; when you hear "Fire" press K (shoot it in his hand),
                       or run away sideways.
- "Thrown! Run sideways now, hold Shift"
                       Dynamite is in the air. Do: Shift + A or D right away.
- "Aiming, wait" / "Steady. Fire."
                       For dynamite: hold L, press K only when it says Fire.
- "2 left", "1 left"   Bullets left in your gun.
- "Empty, press R"     Gun empty. Press R to reload (if you have spare bullets).
- "Empty, no spare ..." No bullets at all for this gun. Use the knife, kicks, grenades,
                       or switch guns.

WHY YOU KEEP DYING HERE
- The Valley sends many enemies at once (4 to 8), and more come after you take
  the emblem. You start with about 10 handgun bullets. That is not enough to shoot
  them all.
- The emblem, the ladder and the crank make you stand still. Enemies grab you then.

WHAT HELPS MOST
1. Use your GRENADES. You carry a Hand Grenade and a Flash Grenade.
   - Flash Grenade: blinds every enemy near you for a few seconds. Then kick them,
     or use those seconds to climb the ladder or turn the crank.
   - Hand Grenade: kills or knocks down a group. Throw it at 3 or more enemies.
   (Ask Claude to add a key that equips and throws them, if that is hard.)
2. Kick instead of shooting: shoot once in the face/leg, then F when it says kick.
3. Back up while fighting (S). Enemies in a line are easier than around you.
4. Do not stand still near enemies. Clear them first, THEN ladder/emblem/crank.
