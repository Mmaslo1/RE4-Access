LAKE MONSTER BATTLE (end of Chapter 3) - how it works with RE4 Access
Written 10/19 by Claude, after fighting it 3 times.

WHAT THE BATTLE IS
You sit in a small boat on the lake. A giant lake monster swims around you.
The boat is tied to the monster with a rope, so it drags you along.
You have NO gun in this fight. Leon throws harpoons (spears) by hand.
That is why you never heard a gun shot: a harpoon throw is almost silent.
Harpoons never run out.
The monster has 2001 health. One harpoon that hits takes about 40 to 360 of it.
It took about 70 throws to win. Many throws miss when it moves or dives.

THE KEYS
  Hold L   = raise a harpoon. The mod turns you toward the monster.
  K        = throw (while you hold L).
  W        = boat forward. A and D = steer left and right.

WHAT THE MOD SAYS
  "In the boat"            = the fight has started, your keys work.
  "Fire, 24 meters"        = you are aimed at the monster. Press K now.
  "Hit"                    = YOUR harpoon hit the monster. Good news.
  "The monster is under water. Steer with W, A and D, and wait."
                           = nothing to throw at. Keep holding L and wait
                             until it comes up and the mod says Fire again.
  "Enemy down"             = you won. A cutscene plays.

THE SIMPLE WAY TO WIN
  1. Hold L.
  2. When you hear "Fire", press K once.
  3. Let go of L for a moment, then hold L again.
  4. Repeat. Over and over, for about 3 to 4 minutes.
  This is exactly what Claude did on the winning try.

HOW YOU CAN LOSE
  Claude died twice, once with the monster at only 10 health left.
  The monster's attacks hurt Leon badly, and the mod does not yet warn you
  before it attacks. Claude does not know yet exactly what the killing attack
  was. Next time it happens, Claude will look into it and add a warning.
  If you die: choose Continue. You start on the shore next to the boat.
  Press N, walk to the boat, press F to get in, and the fight starts again.
  The monster's health is full again after a death.

TIP
  Leon had only 860 of 1260 health when the fight started. Heal before you
  get in the boat (Tab, then a herb or First Aid Spray) so you can take more hits.
