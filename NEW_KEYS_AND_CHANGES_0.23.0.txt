RE4 ACCESS 0.23.0 - NEW KEYS AND CHANGES
Written 1 November 2026. Not yet run in the game.
Do not send the zip to the tester until the test below is done.


NEW KEYS
F1        Reads all the settings and how they are set now.
F2        Combat speech, full or quiet. Yours starts on full.
          Quiet keeps danger warnings, such as grabbed, fire, traps and
          dynamite, plus low health and "Enemy down".
F3        Walking reports on or off. Same as Shift+J.
F4        Short or full messages. Same as Slash.
F5        Automatic or spoken turning. Same as Shift+Period.
F6        Fight sound (the thud) on or off. Yours starts on.
F7        Hit sound on or off. Yours starts off. A short click when you lose health.
F8        How far single-category lists reach: 300, 100 or 60 meters. Yours starts on 300.
Shift+N   While a route is running, says the remaining distance, same as J.
          With no route, it works like N.
          This also applies if you are holding Shift to run.

All your old keys still work. Settings are saved between games.


WHAT THE SOUNDS MEAN
Thud        Not an attack. An enemy is within 7 meters on your floor, or within
            20 meters and in your way. Repeats every 6 seconds.
Buzz        You were grabbed, are burning, or are caught in a bear trap.
            Now 0.3 seconds, was 0.5.
Click       You just lost health. Only if F7 is on.


WHAT CHANGED IN THE GAME
- Report keys say nothing during cutscenes and in the shop.
- In the pause menu they also say nothing, except Y, which repeats the last speech.
- "Not in control" is gone.
- The "game is paused" message for N, Backslash and L is gone.
  Holding W in the pause menu still gives you a message.
- Keys are not silenced in the inventory window yet.
- The mod now speaks through NVDA when NVDA is running and JAWS is not.
  JAWS still comes first whenever it is running.


WHAT CHANGED ON YOUR COMPUTER
- The mod's files in the game folder were updated.
- The speech plugin now supports NVDA.
- NVDA's helper file, nvdaControllerClient64.dll, is now in the game folder.
- Added in reframework\data: the hit sound, the key file instructions, his
  layout file, and a backup of the old grab sound.
- Your saves, your NVDA and JAWS settings, and the game's own files were not touched.


THE TEST, ABOUT 10 MINUTES, WITH NVDA
Start up:
1. Press Ctrl+Alt+N to start NVDA. If nothing happens, open NVDA from the Start menu.
2. Press Insert+F4 to close JAWS.
3. Start the game from Steam and load your Chapter 6 save.
   If NVDA reads the menus, that part passes.

In the game:
1. Press F1. It should read the list of settings.
2. Press F2. It should say "Quiet combat". Near enemies you should hear only
   danger warnings and "Enemy down", plus the thud. Press F2 again for full speech.
3. Press F7. You hear the click. Take a hit and you should hear it again.
   Press F7 again to turn it off.
4. If an enemy grabs you, the buzz should be shorter.
5. Press Escape, then H, N and U. Those keys should say nothing.
   Y should still repeat the last speech. Close the menu.
6. Press N to start a route, then Shift+N. It should say the remaining distance.
7. If a cutscene plays, press H, N and U. The mod should stay silent.
   If none comes up, skip this one.

When you are done:
1. Close the game.
2. Press Insert+Q and then Enter to close NVDA.
3. Start JAWS again.
4. Tell Claude which steps failed, if any. Claude reads the log.
