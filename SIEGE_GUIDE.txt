SIEGE IN LUIS'S HOUSE - "Stand your ground" (Chapter 5)
Won live by Claude on 10/22/2026 (Assisted difficulty), about 5 minutes. This is what worked.

NUMBER KEYS (checked by pressing them; the mod says the list when the siege starts)
  1 = SG-09 R handgun
  2 = Flash Grenade
  3 = W-870 shotgun
  4 = Hand Grenade
After you pick up more grenades they go on the same keys.

BEFORE YOU GO IN (at the checkpoint outside the house)
1. Heal to full. Tab opens the case. E switches to Craft. Pick "Mixed Herb (G+R+Y)"
   (Green + Red + Yellow) and HOLD Enter about 2 seconds. Then Escape, Tab,
   go to the Mixed Herb, Enter, Use. It heals fully and raises your maximum health.
2. Walk into the house with N. A cutscene starts the siege.

DURING THE SIEGE
- There is no way out. You win by surviving until a cutscene ends it (about 5 minutes).
  A second short cutscene comes in the middle; keep going after it.
- Villagers come in at once, from all sides. Do NOT walk around: stand and fight.
- Hold L (the mod locks onto the nearest enemy), press K again and again.
  Press F after each hit: when an enemy is stunned, F is a kick that knocks a group down.
- When 3 or more are close but not touching you: press 4, hold L, press K to throw
  a Hand Grenade. Then press 1 to go back to the handgun.
- When 2 or more are right on you: press 3 for the shotgun. Press 1 after.
- When you hear "Empty, press R": press R.
- When you hear "Grabbed! Space": tap Space.
- When health is low (about a third): press Tab. The game PAUSES while the case is open,
  so take your time: use a healing item (the Chicken Egg works), Escape back.
- Pick up what enemies drop (F when the mod says "F ready: Item"): ammo and herbs.
- Luis fights with you and helps when you are grabbed: stay near him.

THE WINDOWS (optional)
Wooden Planks lie on the table at the start. F takes them (only when no enemy is touching
you). Then F at a window opens a list: pick Wooden Planks, Use. You can't do it while an
enemy is at that window. A blocked window only holds for a short time, so fighting matters
more. N during the siege walks you to planks or a window only when no enemy is within 4 m.

AFTER
The chapter end screen: Shift opens the Typewriter. The mod now says the selected item
("Save Game, 1 of 3") and each save slot ("Slot 9 of 20, empty"). Pick an empty slot.
