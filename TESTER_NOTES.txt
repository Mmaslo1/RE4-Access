RE4 ACCESS - TEST BUILD NOTES

This is a test build of a work in progress. Thanks for looking at it.

HOW FAR IT GOES
Version 0.26.0: the mod has been played through Chapter 8 (castle, Bindery
stone table, the tower levers, the Armored Gigante and the cannon). Chapter 9
(the hedge maze) is being mapped next. Chapter 8 tips: rocks from the giant only
hit on the top floor; on the cannon follow the Left/Right/Up/Down calls and
press K on "On target".

WHAT WORKS AND WHAT DOES NOT YET
- NEW in 0.25.3, from your feedback: on an N route, a stop chime (two bell
  notes, high then low) plays where F is needed, and that "Stop here ... press F"
  line is protected for a few seconds, so ordinary speech no longer cuts it off.
  Danger warnings and fights still interrupt. Not tested in game yet: please
  say if the chime is too often, too loud, or if anything important waits too long.
- Documents picked up in the world are read aloud with F. Y repeats them.
- The Files screen reader, the Minus key (missed documents) and Shift+Minus
  (requests) are new. See the Documents and Requests part of the README.
- Picture-only documents and treasure maps have no spoken descriptions yet.
- Optional treasures are in the mod's lists, but are not announced on their own.
- Chapter 6 chase: tested and passed. Mendez boss: beaten by the owner with the
  mod's spoken warnings (MENDEZ_BOSS_GUIDE). New sounds: a high double beep for
  the game's Evade prompt, a low beep for Move. Evade says E, which is not yet
  confirmed as the working key on the keyboard.

SPEECH
The mod speaks through JAWS. Without JAWS it uses the Windows voice (SAPI).
There is no direct NVDA support yet, so NVDA users get the Windows voice.

THE SOURCE IS OPEN
All of the mod is plain Lua, in reframework\autorun. The speech plugin source
is in the source folder. Change or add whatever you like and send it back.

THIS BUILD HAS NO UNLIMITED AMMO
The owner's own copy has a Shift+X unlimited ammo option. It only switches on
if the file reframework\data\REAccess_owner.txt exists, and that file is not
in this package. The same file also turns on a developer command channel,
so that channel is off here too.

LANGUAGES
Speech comes from reframework\data\REAccess_lang_<language>.json, named after the
game's display language: REAccess_lang_German.json, and so on. Copy
REAccess_lang_English.json, rename it, and fill in the right side of each line.
{1} and {2} stand for names and numbers and may change order. Anything empty stays
English and is listed in reframework\data\REAccess_lang_missing.txt. To force a
language, put one word in reframework\data\REAccess_language.txt.
No translations ship yet. Objective routes now work in any game language. Some
checks still use English item names (herb notes, some key-item steps), so a few
hints may be missing in another language.

WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Send the log file: re2_framework_log.txt, in the game folder. Lines from the
mod start with [RE4Access]. Please say what you heard and what you pressed.

NEW IN 0.23.0 (from your feedback)
SETTINGS ON FUNCTION KEYS (new in 0.23.0, saved between games)
F1   Says all the settings below and how they are set now.
F2   Combat speech: full or quiet. Quiet keeps only danger (grabbed, fire, traps, dynamite, low health) and "Enemy down". Off by default.
F3   Walking reports on or off (same as Shift+J).
F4   Short or full messages (same as Slash).
F5   Automatic or spoken turning (same as Shift+Period).
F6   Fight sound on or off.
F7   Hit sound on or off. Off by default. A short click when you lose health.
F8   How far the lists reach for single categories: 300, 100 or 60 meters. The Everything list stays 60.
Shift+N: while a route runs, the same as J. With no route it works like N.
Keys stay silent during cutscenes, in the shop and in the pause menu (only Y works in the pause menu).

WHAT THE SOUNDS MEAN
Fight sound (a thud): NOT an attack. An enemy is within 7 meters on your floor, or within 20 meters and in your way. Repeats every 6 seconds.
Grab buzz: you were grabbed, are burning, or are caught in a bear trap.
Hit click (F7, if turned on): you just lost health.

YOUR OWN KEYS
